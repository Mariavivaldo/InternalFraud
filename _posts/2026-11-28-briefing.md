    title: Former crypto exchange-shop clerk admits taking bribes to divert nearly HK$9 million in client USDT trades
    summary: Hung Pak-hin, 31, pleaded guilty in an ICAC case to trading with the shop's clients through outsiders' wallets for HK$5,600; sentencing is on December 11.
    detail: >-
      Hung Pak-hin (洪栢鍁), 31, a former customer service and operations assistant at a
      cryptocurrency over-the-counter exchange shop, pleaded guilty at Fanling Magistrates' Courts on
      November 27, 2026 to one count of conspiracy for an agent to accept an advantage, under the
      Prevention of Bribery Ordinance and the Crimes Ordinance, in a case brought by the ICAC.
      Between August and November 2023 he accepted HK$5,600 from two men and used cryptocurrency
      wallets they supplied to trade privately with the shop's clients, so that cash trades in more
      than 1.1 million USDT, worth nearly HK$9 million, went to the two men's wallets instead of his
      employer's. Most reports name the employer as 一宗貿易（香港）有限公司; one report gives a
      different Chinese name for the defendant. The magistrate remanded him in custody and adjourned
      sentencing to December 11 for background reports. The ICAC charged him on September 30, 2026
      and said the company had assisted fully in the investigation. No ICAC release on the plea was
      found, and the articles could not be opened from this session, so these details come from them
      as shown in search results.
