    detail: >-
      The Nassau County Sheriff's Office in Florida announced on Friday, November 20, 2026 the
      arrest of Corey James Sullivan, 41, of St. Marys, Georgia, a former senior relationship
      banker at the Truist branch on South Fletcher Avenue in Fernandina Beach on Amelia Island.
      According to the arrest warrant as reported, seven transactions between May 5 and July 10
      withdrew a total of $26,500 from a customer's account using the customer's personal
      information, including account numbers, and each was followed days later by smaller cash
      deposits into Sullivan's own account at a Truist ATM. Other staff noticed the withdrawals,
      the bank reported the theft to the sheriff's office on August 24, and Truist fired Sullivan
      after an internal investigation. He faces 17 charges, including identity theft, grand theft
      from a person 65 or older, organized scheme to defraud, unlawful use of a two-way
      communication device and uttering forged instruments, and was booked into the Nassau County
      jail on a bond of more than $650,000. The customer recovered the money but had to pay legal
      fees, the sheriff's office said. Detectives believe there may be more victims and are asking
      anyone who thinks their account was affected to contact them. These are allegations. The
      sheriff's office release was not found, and the articles could not be opened from this
