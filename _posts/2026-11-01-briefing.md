    title: FSS gives NH Nonghyup Bank an institutional warning and sanctions 20 employees for falsifying inspection records
    summary: Staff altered about 27,800 computer records and submitted them to inspectors during 2024 and 2025 inspections, the FSS says.
    detail: >-
      The Financial Supervisory Service disclosed on October 30 that it had given NH Nonghyup
      Bank an institutional warning, a heavy sanction, for organised obstruction of its
      inspections, DailyBrief reported. The FSS also notified sanctions, including pay cuts and
      reprimands, for 20 bank employees. The article's subheading puts the pay cuts at six and the
      reprimands at seven. According to the FSS, during a 2025 inspection of the bank's
      anti-money-laundering work, staff manipulated computer entries and submitted false
      customer-due-diligence data. They also left out records of customer checks done through
      third parties, and restricted system access so inspectors could not see that material had
      been falsified or omitted. The FSS says the bank also submitted falsified customer-check data
      during its 2024 regular inspection, and left out documents the FSS requested in March 2025.
      In total, about 27,800 computer records were altered and submitted as inspection material.
      Other findings include failures to carry out customer due diligence and to report large cash
      transactions. The FSS also issued nine requests for improvement. The FSS release itself could
      not be opened from this session, so this item relies on the press report. The publication
      date is taken from search results.

  - id: sg-mas-prohibition-orders-false-trading
    region: sg
    category: Enforcement
    title: MAS bans four men convicted in false-trading schemes, including two former finance-industry representatives
    summary: Market maker Huang Yiwen gets 13 years; Tay Sze Chien 7 years and Tan Wei Liang 4 years; former trading representative Sim Teck Chye 3 years.
    detail: >-
      The Monetary Authority of Singapore issued prohibition orders on October 29, 2026 under the
      Financial Services and Markets Act against four men convicted of offences under the
      Securities and Futures Act. MAS found that none of them is a fit and proper person. Huang
      Yiwen, who ran a commercial market maker, is banned for 13 years. Tay Sze Chien gets 7 years
      and Tan Wei Liang 4 years. Police describe Tay as a fund manager and Tan as a licensed trading
      representative at the time. Between September 2017 and February 2020 they conspired with
      Huang to create a misleading appearance of active trading in five thinly traded SGX-listed
      stocks. The scheme used cross trades that let Tay profit, and Tan joined later. MAS says Tay
      and Tan received benefits of about S$343,946 and S$122,802 respectively. Tan was sentenced to
      six weeks' jail after pleading guilty to one count of false trading and one count of
      unauthorised trading. Sim Teck Chye, a former trading representative who later worked as an
      investor relations executive at AGV Group, is banned for 3 years. He relayed the AGV chief
      executive's instructions to Huang, who placed orders to move AGV's share price between
      November 2017 and January 2018. Huang was jailed in August 2025. The MAS order was published
      on October 29 and had not been covered in earlier editions. No press coverage was found.
