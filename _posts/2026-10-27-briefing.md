    title: DFSA fines former DIFC firm executive $210,000 and bars him for hiding past fraud convictions from investigators
    summary: Gary Joseph Peters falsely denied earlier dishonesty convictions in a compulsory interview; his tribunal challenge was struck out.
    detail: >-
      The Dubai Financial Services Authority fined Gary Joseph Peters $210,000 (Dh771,330) and
      barred him from holding office in, or being employed by, any DFSA-authorised firm or fund,
      and from any financial-services function in or from the DIFC. Peters worked for a
      DFSA-authorised firm from November 2020 to June 2022. His contract described him as chief
      operating officer, and his main role was introducing investors. He was never a
      DFSA-authorised individual. In a compulsory interview he falsely denied having been
      convicted of offences involving fraud or dishonesty, although he had pleaded guilty in
      three criminal cases in another jurisdiction, breaching Article 66 of the Regulatory Law
      2004. He also failed to produce certificates for qualifications he claimed. The Decision
      Notice is dated May 21, 2026. Peters referred it to the Financial Markets Tribunal on June
      18, and the tribunal struck out the reference on October 19, which made the action public.
