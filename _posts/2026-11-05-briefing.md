    summary: Prosecutors say the ring targeted nearly 3,000 victims whose accounts held more than $373 million; the alleged leader was arrested at JFK boarding a one-way flight to Malaysia.
    detail: >-
      A federal grand jury in the Northern District of Texas returned a 22-count indictment in
      August against 14 defendants. It was unsealed on Tuesday, November 3, after the arrest of
      co-defendant Terrence Dudley. The charges include conspiracy to commit bank fraud and wire
      fraud, bank fraud, wire fraud and aggravated identity theft. Prosecutors say the ring
      included corrupt insiders at major banking and telecommunications companies. It allegedly
      used them, together with SIM swaps, spoofed calls and fake identification, to get past
      account security and take over customers' accounts across the country. The alleged
      leader, Muhamed Dieye, 35, of Dallas, kept a spreadsheet of nearly 3,000 victims whose
      accounts held more than $373 million in all. That is the balance of the targeted accounts,
      not the amount stolen. Reports say he was arrested at John F. Kennedy International Airport
      while trying to board a one-way flight to Malaysia. The indictment also describes an
      attempted $2 million wire from a Charles Schwab account to Emirates Islamic Bank in March
      2024. The reports seen do not say which defendants, if any, worked at a bank. The
      prosecutors' release and the press reports could not be opened from this session, so these
      details come from them as shown in search results. These are allegations, not proven facts.

    detail: >-
      The ICAC charged Geoffrey Tong Po-hang (湯寶衡), 39, a former vice-president of Hong Kong
      Exchanges and Clearing, and his mother, Leung Yiu-chun (梁耀珍), 68, each with one count of
      obtaining access to a computer with a view to dishonest gain, Hong Kong media reported on
      November 4. Prosecutors allege that between February 2018 and April 2023 Tong dishonestly
      used HKEX's internal information systems several times to view confidential information on
      listed companies that had nothing to do with his duties. His mother's investment account
      then traded shares in those companies, making profits of more than HK$500,000. The case
      came from a corruption complaint referred by the Securities and Futures Commission. The ICAC
      and the SFC worked closely on it, and HKEX assisted. The two appeared at Eastern Magistrates'
      Courts on the afternoon of November 4 (case ESCC2590/2026). The case was adjourned to February
      17, 2027, and bail of HK$150,000 and HK$50,000 was set for the two. The ICAC says its
      investigation continues and further charges are possible. In September the ICAC separately
      charged Tong with failing to comply with a notice to declare his assets. HKEX runs the
      city's exchanges and clearing houses. The ICAC release and the press reports could not be
      opened from this session, so these details come from the press reports as shown in search
      results. These are allegations, not proven facts.

    detail: >-
      The anti-corruption investigation unit of the Seoul Metropolitan Police Agency's
      metropolitan investigation division applied on November 3 to the Seoul Central District
      prosecutors for an arrest warrant for Kang Ho-dong, chairman of the National Agricultural
      Cooperative Federation (Nonghyup), Korean media reported on November 4. The warrant
      application cites bribery under the Act on the Aggravated Punishment of Specific Crimes. It
      alleges Kang received about ₩100 million in cash and valuables from the head of a services
      vendor that does business with Nonghyup affiliates around the January 2024 federation
      chairman election. It also cites a breach of the Improper Solicitation and Graft Act over
      condolence money worth several hundred million won that he allegedly accepted at his
      mother-in-law's funeral. Police are separately still looking into the alleged misuse of
      about ₩240 million in federation funds, and Kang's secretary has already been arrested for
      occupational embezzlement in that part of the case. Police raided the federation in October
      2025, barred Kang from leaving the country and questioned him several times this year, most
      recently on October 29. As of November 4, prosecutors had not yet decided whether to ask a
      court for the warrant and were expected to decide after questioning Kang. Kang has denied
      the gift-fund allegations. The reports could not be opened from this session, so these
      details come from them as shown in search results. These are allegations, not proven facts.
