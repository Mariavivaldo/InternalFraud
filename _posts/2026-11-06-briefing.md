    title: Prosecutors decline to seek an arrest warrant for Nonghyup Federation chairman Kang Ho-dong, citing insufficient evidence
    summary: The public prosecution office says the bribery allegations are not shown clearly enough and detention is not needed after an investigation of more than 18 months.
    detail: >-
      The first non-referral review division of the Seoul Central District public prosecution
      office (공소청) said on November 6 that it had turned down the police applications for
      arrest warrants for Kang Ho-dong, chairman of the National Agricultural Cooperative
      Federation (Nonghyup), and for the head of a services vendor, identified only as A. The
      anti-corruption unit of the Seoul Metropolitan Police Agency had applied on November 3. The
      office said that, after reviewing the police evidence and a pre-warrant interview with the
      suspects on November 5, the main allegations were not shown clearly enough to justify a
      warrant. It added that detention was not necessary or proportionate at this stage, given an
      investigation lasting more than 18 months and the suspects' attendance when summoned. This
      is not a court ruling; the office simply decided not to take the application to court. Police
      accuse Kang of taking more than ₩100 million in money and valuables from the vendor's head
      around the January 2024 federation election. They are also looking into about ₩240 million
      in federation funds allegedly spent on gifts such as red ginseng and cosmetics, and into
      large condolence payments Kang allegedly received from current and former Nonghyup staff at
      his mother-in-law's funeral in May 2025. Kang's side says the condolence money was not given in
      return for anything. Police are expected to consider further investigation and a new
      application. One earlier report on November 6 said the office had asked the court for the
      warrant; several later reports from different outlets say it did not. The reports could not
      be opened from this session, so these details come from them as shown in search results.
      These are allegations, not proven facts.
