    title: KOOPJB cooperative manager fined RM30,000 for falsifying her own personal financing application
    summary: The Johor Bahru Sessions Court fined Ku Siti Zulaikha Mohamed Zakkariya, 39, on November 17 after she admitted using false details to obtain RM56,000 of financing she was not eligible for.
    detail: >-
      Ku Siti Zulaikha Mohamed Zakkariya, 39, a manager at Koperasi Pegawai Kerajaan Daerah Johor
      Bahru Berhad (KOOPJB), was fined RM30,000, or six months' jail in default, by the Johor Bahru
      Sessions Court on November 17, 2026, according to Berita Harian, Utusan Malaysia, RTM and
      Buletin TV3. She had pleaded guilty to cheating under Section 417 of the Penal Code, which
      carries up to five years' jail, a fine or both; some reports describe it as an alternative
      charge. According to the facts read in court, on September 23, 2022 at the cooperative's
      office she applied for RM56,000 of Bai' Al-Inah personal financing although she was eligible
      for only about RM33,938.22 net. Her outstanding debts, credit use and arrears records in the
      application were also altered. The cooperative's chairman, 61, approved the financing without
      realising the figures had been changed, relying on its risk rating. The defence cited a
      first offence and her RM4,000 monthly income. The prosecution said she abused a position that
      demands trust, honesty and integrity. The case was brought by the Malaysian Anti-Corruption
      Commission. Reports differ slightly on the bail amount and on when the plea was recorded. No
      official release was found, and the reports could not be opened from this session, so these
      details come from them as shown in search results.
