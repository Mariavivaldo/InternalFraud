    region: hk
    category: Insider & employee fraud
    title: Former Standard Chartered relationship manager jailed for 45 months over fake bank documents in ¥400 million investment fraud
    summary: Leung Ho-yin and a financial consultant are jailed after guilty pleas, and an investment-company manager gets 54 months for laundering about HK$55 million.
    detail: >-
      On November 9 the District Court sentenced the last three defendants in a case brought by
      the ICAC. Leung Ho-yin, then a relationship manager at Standard Chartered Bank (Hong Kong),
      was jailed for 45 months. Law Man-fai, a self-employed financial consultant, was jailed for
      33 months. Both had pleaded guilty to conspiracy to defraud. The judge took 68 months as the
      starting point for both and gave Law a larger discount for helping the prosecution. Catherine
      Kum Kit-ching, a manager of ADF Capital Limited, was convicted after trial of conspiring with
      two foreign men to launder about HK$55 million in crime proceeds through a bank account. She
      was jailed for 54 months.


      Between 2015 and 2016 the group used proof-of-funds letters and corporate refund promissory
      notes that claimed to come from Standard Chartered. The documents said several companies held
      more than HK$37 billion in their bank accounts and that the bank stood behind ADF. With them,
      the group persuaded Japanese investors to put more than ¥400 million (about HK$28.4 million)
      into the scheme. The bank had not issued the documents. Two other then Standard Chartered
      relationship managers, Woo Man-ho and Chan Tak-ching, who signed the documents, were each
      jailed for three years in August. The ICAC is still seeking three foreign nationals. Reports
      differ on Leung's age (39 or 40) and on the court: on.cc names West Kowloon Magistrates'
      Courts, while the other outlets say the District Court.
