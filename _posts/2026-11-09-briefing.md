    region: kr
    category: Insider & employee fraud
    title: Bank employees among 13 arrested in Incheon over ₩181.3bn in loans taken out with inflated contracts on unsold commercial units
    summary: Police catch 185 people, including two current or former bankers and a tax accountant, over mortgage loans from 12 lenders.
    detail: >-
      The Incheon Metropolitan Police Agency's wide-area crime investigation unit said on
      November 9 that it had caught 185 people suspected of obtaining about ₩181.3 billion in
      mortgage loans from 12 financial institutions between 2023 and February 2026. Police
      arrested 13 of them, including two current or former bank employees, A (52) and B (56),
      and a tax accountant, C (54). Police say the bankers led the scheme or helped carry out many
      of the loans. The other 172, among them loan brokers and nominal borrowers, were booked
      without detention. They face aggravated fraud charges under the Act on the Aggravated
      Punishment of Specific Economic Crimes.


      Police say the ring recruited people with no money of their own as nominal buyers of
      long-unsold commercial units and officetels in Incheon and the Seoul area. It promised them
      cash of about 10% of each loan, and drew up "up" contracts with sale prices above the real
      price so that the loans would be larger. Down payments were paid on the buyers' behalf and
      then taken back, so the buyers appeared to have put in their own money. The developer, sales
      agency, brokers and borrowers then split the loans. Reports put the number of units at about
      250 to 370. Police started the case after a tip that units listed as sold had stood empty for
      long periods with no fit-out. The banks the employees worked for have not been named.
