    detail: >-
      The Federal Reserve Board announced on November 12, 2026 a consent prohibition order against
      Katilyn M. Robinson, a former employee of United Community Bank, Greenville, South Carolina, a
      state member bank. The release gives the reason as misappropriation of customer funds.
      According to press coverage of the order, Robinson worked as a teller at the bank's Murphy,
      North Carolina branch, took about $42,830 in cash held in the branch vault and teller drawers
      between roughly 2024 and June 2025, resigned in June 2025 and was later convicted of
      embezzlement under North Carolina law. The order, issued under Section 8(e) of the Federal
      Deposit Insurance Act, bars her from taking part in the affairs of any federally insured
      depository institution without prior written approval from the Board and, where needed,
      another federal regulator. She consented to it and waived a hearing and judicial review. In
      August 2026 the Fed issued a similar order against another former United Community Bank
      teller, Crystal A. Wykle. The Federal Reserve site and the press reports could not be opened
      from this session, so these details come from copies of the release and from coverage as
      shown in search results.

  - id: kr-gold-and-s-stock-manipulation-securities-employees
    region: kr
    category: Insider & employee fraud
    title: Securities firm employees among 25 sent to prosecutors over ₩6 billion Gold&S stock manipulation
    summary: Seoul police say brokerage employees placed manipulative orders from branch terminals in a scheme led by former gangsters that made about ₩6 billion.
    detail: >-
      The Seoul Metropolitan Police Agency's financial crime investigation unit said on November 12
      that it had sent 25 people to prosecutors, without detention, for manipulating the share price
      of KOSDAQ-listed Gold&S. Police say that over about eight months, from October 2022 to June
      2023, the group used 147 personal and corporate nominee accounts to place 9,396 manipulative
      orders, trading about 31 million shares worth about ₩120 billion. Two former gangsters from
      Busan allegedly planned and led the scheme and made about ₩3 billion each, ₩6 billion in
      total. Employees of a securities firm were brought in as "players"; entrusted with the group's
      trading, they placed manipulative orders through the firm's branch terminals. A moneylender
      who supplied funds and the chief executive of another listed company also took part. The
      share price rose roughly threefold before a member of the group sold in bulk; forced selling
      of shares pledged for loans followed, and the stock hit its daily lower limit four days in a
      row. Police found no evidence that Gold&S insiders took part. Arrest warrants for three core
      suspects were not granted. Reports differ on the number of nominee accounts (140-odd, 147 or
      157) and on the size of the price rise. The securities firm was not named in the reports
      found. The reports could not be opened from this session, so these details come from them as
      shown in search results. These are allegations, not proven facts.
