    summary: The SFC found he misrepresented his degrees in licence applications from 2011 to 2022 and gave it a false degree certificate.

      The Securities and Futures Commission announced on November 17, 2026 that it has banned Kim
      Chung Wan from re-entering the industry for life. Kim was licensed at various times between
      2011 and 2023 to deal in securities, advise on securities and carry on asset management; his
      last licence ended in August 2023 and he is not currently licensed. The SFC found that he
      misrepresented his academic qualifications to it on several occasions in support of licence

      2022, he misrepresented his qualifications to former employers in three job applications. The
      SFC said this breached section 383 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance and that he is not
      fit and proper to be a regulated person. The reports seen do not name his former employers.
      The SFC release and the articles could not be opened from this session, so these details come

  - id: kr-korea-insurance-finance-rebates
    region: kr
    category: Enforcement
    title: FSS sends registration-cancellation pre-notice to insurance agency 한국보험금융 over ₩73.2 billion in illegal rebates
    summary: Police referred 231 people, including a branch operator accused of breach of trust that cost the agency about ₩22.4 billion; the agency had until November 19 to respond.
    detail: >-
      The Financial Supervisory Service has sent a pre-notice of registration cancellation, its
      heaviest sanction, to the insurance general agency (GA) 한국보험금융 (Korea Insurance Finance),
      a top-10 GA with more than 6,000 insurance planners, Korean media reported on November 16 and
      17, 2026. The Seoul Metropolitan Police Agency's investigation unit has referred 231 people to
      prosecutors: a branch operator identified as A, 26 members of his organisation, 46 corporate
      representatives who allegedly received rebates, and 158 insurance planners who allegedly lent
      their names so that sales commissions could be split. A's group is accused of paying ₩73.2
      billion in cash rebates to 505 corporate representatives who took out policies, which is
      illegal under the Insurance Business Act. When rebate money ran short, the group allegedly had
      410 policies cancelled early, causing the agency's head office a loss of about ₩22.4 billion,
      which police treat as breach of trust; they are also accused of evading about ₩22.9 billion in
      income tax. The agency said no head-office executives or staff are among those referred and
      that the cancellation is only at the pre-notice stage. It was reported to have until November
      19 to answer the FSS, and no final sanction has been decided. These are allegations. The
      articles could not be opened from this session, so these details come from them as shown in
      search results.
