    detail: >-
      Luis Antonio Rego Bairos-Pirelli, 41, of Lowell, Massachusetts, an assistant bank manager,
      was charged on November 17, 2026 with receipt of a bribe by a bank official, the US
      Attorney's Office for the District of Massachusetts announced. Prosecutors allege that from
      around 2023 a check casher paid him about $1,000 in cash a week, more than $50,000 in all. In
      return he allegedly processed the check casher's checks for deposit even when the bank's
      anti-fraud software flagged them, and made the cash available for immediate withdrawal in
      breach of bank policy. More than $28 million of the checks bounced between February and April
      2026, according to the charging papers, leaving the bank with a loss of more than $1.5
      million. He appeared in federal court in Boston on November 17 and was released on
      conditions. The charge carries up to 30 years in prison and a fine of up to $1 million or
      three times the bribes. The reports seen do not name the bank. A charge is only an
      allegation. The release and the Boston Globe report could not be opened from this session,
      so these details come from them as shown in search results.
