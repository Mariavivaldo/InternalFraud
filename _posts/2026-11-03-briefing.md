    summary: Prosecutors say the owner of Corporate America Lending deceived a loan buyer over a $58 million loan, took out loans in customers' names and sold investors interests in loans that did not exist.
    detail: >-
      Ron Cook, 58, of Fresno, owner of the agricultural lender Corporate America Lending, has been
      indicted on charges of making false statements to the federal government, wire fraud,
      aggravated identity theft and money laundering, local outlets reported on November 2,
      citing the US Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of California. He was arrested at
      his office. Prosecutors allege that he took nearly $65 million from agricultural borrowers,
      investors and financial companies, including the Federal Agricultural Mortgage Corp. (Farmer
      Mac). The largest part of the alleged scheme began in 2023. Borrowers on a $58 million loan
      that Cook had originated and sold to a financial company refinanced it with another
      company. Prosecutors say Cook misrepresented the refinancing to obtain the $58 million
      without the first company's knowledge, then moved the money through friends and associates
      to hide it. He is also accused of using customers' identities to take out Farmer Mac loans
      in their names without their knowledge, and of selling investors interests in loans that did
      not exist. Prosecutors say he used the money for his lifestyle, to repay customers and
      investors who discovered the fraud, and for political donations. The FBI and the FDA Office
      of Criminal Investigations investigated. The prosecutors' release could not be opened from
      this session, so this item relies on local press. These are allegations, not proven facts.
