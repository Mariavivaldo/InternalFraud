    title: FSS starts first unannounced bank-branch inspections in a decade at Shinhan, Woori, NH Nonghyup and IBK
    summary: Inspectors arrive before opening to check vaults, cash on hand and key certificates, after financial accidents at the five largest banks rose from 21 in 2021 to 75 in 2025.
    detail: >-
      The Financial Supervisory Service began unannounced on-site inspections on November 16, 2026
      at one or two high-risk branches each of Shinhan Bank, Woori Bank, NH Nonghyup Bank and IBK
      Industrial Bank of Korea, according to multiple Korean reports. It is the first time since
      2015, when the FSS largely dropped broad branch-level inspections, that it has inspected
      individual branches rather than head offices without advance notice. According to the
      reports, the inspection team arrives before the branch opens, presents an inspection order and
      checks the vault, cash on hand and the management of important certificates, which are the
      controls that catch employee embezzlement. It will also test whether branch loan procedures
      work and can screen out loan fraud using forged documents such as fake sale contracts. Each
      branch is to be inspected for five business days, with extensions possible, and the FSS may
      widen the exercise to other banks or sectors depending on the results. The reports put the
      number of financial accidents at KB Kookmin, Shinhan, Hana, Woori and NH Nonghyup at 21 in
      2021, 25 in 2022, 26 in 2023, 55 in 2024 and 75 in 2025. IBK, Shinhan and Woori recently
      disclosed large loan-fraud losses involving outsiders; outlets differ on the amounts. An FSS
      official declined to officially confirm the inspections. The reports could not be opened from
      this session, so these details come from them as shown in search results.
