    detail: >-
      The fifth criminal division of the Busan District Court sentenced a Hana Bank employee,
      identified only as A, to three years in prison on November 11. A was convicted of aggravated
      embezzlement under the Act on the Aggravated Punishment of Specific Economic Crimes,
      occupational embezzlement, forgery of private documents and use of forged documents. Between
      December 2020 and March 2024, while handling foreign-exchange and lending work at a Busan-area
      branch, A embezzled about ₩1.39 billion. The court found that A took customers' currency
      exchange money and obtained loans in client companies' names with forged documents, using
      passbooks, seals and account passwords that customers had left with the branch for
      convenience. To hide the losses, A forged and used documents 12 times, including altering
      loan balances on client companies' debt certificates. The court cited the seriousness of the
      offences, the roughly ₩480 million still unpaid and the lack of forgiveness from victims.
      Reports note that one person handled foreign-exchange and lending work for years without
      cross-checks. The reports could not be opened from this session, so these details come from
