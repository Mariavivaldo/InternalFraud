    region: kr
    category: Insider & employee fraud
    title: Former bank employee jailed for a year for diverting a customer's ₩100m loan repayment into a personal "loan"
    summary: The employee told the customer not to repay the bank but to lend the money to the employee for two months; most of it was never repaid.
    detail: >-
      Judge Kwon Soon-beom of the Namyangju branch of the Uijeongbu District Court (Criminal
      Single Division 4) sentenced a former bank employee in their 50s, identified only as A, to one
      year in prison for fraud, Financial News reported on November 1. Prosecutors say that in late
      January 2023, at a bank branch in Dongdaemun-gu, Seoul, where A then worked, A approached
      customer B, who had come in to repay a loan. A told B not to repay the loan yet but to lend
      the ₩100 million to A instead, promising to pay it back in two months and claiming to be
      able to afford it. In fact A was already in default at the time, and never repaid the money.
      In A's favour, the court weighed that A admitted the offence, showed remorse and had paid
      back part of the money. Against A, it noted that most of the loss has not been recovered and
      that A has not settled with the victim. The reports describe this as a first-instance
      ruling. The bank is not named, and the sentencing date is not given. No court release was
      found, and the articles could not be opened from this session, so these details come from
      the press reports as shown in search results.
