    summary: Pennsylvania State Police say Amanda Larue Chandler made out withdrawal slips on customers' accounts during busy drive-thru periods and pocketed the cash.
    detail: >-
      Pennsylvania State Police at Clearfield charged Amanda Larue Chandler, 39, of Houtzdale, a
      teller at a Mid Penn Bank branch in Clearfield County, with 22 counts of theft by unlawful
      taking and 22 counts of forgery, local outlets reported on October 29. According to the
      criminal complaint, between December 5, 2025 and August 14, 2026 she waited until the
      drive-thru line was busy, pulled up customers' account details at the teller desk, and
      generated withdrawal slips in their names. She allegedly hid the cash in her drawer and took
      it out at the end of her shift. Police put the total at about $32,300 taken from nine
      customers, one of whom lost $14,750. The case came to light when a customer told the bank's
      security director that money was missing. The bank has reimbursed the customers and is
      seeking restitution. Reports differ on how the forgery counts are graded. No police or court
      release was found, so this item relies on local press.
