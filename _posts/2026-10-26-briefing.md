    title: Audit data shows ₩20.7bn in securities-firm financial accidents last year, led by employee theft and breach of trust
    summary: Korea Investment & Securities lost ₩3.1bn of client money to theft; a DB Securities employee's breach of trust ran almost nine years.
    detail: >-
      According to Financial Supervisory Service data released through People Power Party
      lawmakers during the 2026 National Assembly audit, securities firms reported 14 financial
      accidents worth ₩20.738 billion in 2025, down from ₩156.1 billion in 2024. Six more,
      worth ₩7.07 billion, were reported through August 2026: two cases of occupational breach of
      trust (₩2.83 billion) and three of fraud. At Korea Investment & Securities, client money
      worth about ₩3.1 billion was taken, and only about 16% has been recovered. At DB Securities,
      an employee's breach of trust worth about ₩3 billion went on for roughly eight years and
      eight months. In cases cited in the coverage, employees took client money for IPO
      subscriptions and used it to pay personal debts, and a staffer signed an overseas
      investment that internal review had rejected. Since 2017, the sector's accidents total
      ₩295.3 billion, and Shinhan Investment accounts for 51.7% of that.

      Financial Supervisory Service data released during the 2026 National Assembly audit shows

      insurers, one at a non-life insurer). At KB Insurance, an employee at the
      individual-endorsement support center embezzled long-unclaimed surrender refunds owed on
      policies of deceased insureds between July 2024 and March 2025. The loss is put at ₩1.612
      billion, of which ₩180 million (about 11.2%) has been recovered. The company first put the
      figure at about ₩1.4 billion when it disclosed the case, and it has filed a police
      complaint. Separately, a Meritz Fire planner diverted about ₩1.6 billion in premiums
      collected from customers and has been permanently barred from dealing with the company. A
      former Mirae Asset Life planner failed to return about ₩2.7 billion that customers handed
      over for investment.
